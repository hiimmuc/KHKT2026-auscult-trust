### Evaluation boundary for the 150-fit campaign

The 150 fits cover six arms × five ICBHI training-patient folds × five seeds. They provide source-CV estimates only. For **SWA, LoRA, multi-scale, class-aware light, and LoRA + class-aware**, all three next endpoints remain unrun:

| Endpoint | Next measurement | Interpretation if completed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official | Retrain each recipe at its source-selected epoch; score the official test split with paired seeds. | Tests held-out benchmark performance. |
| LODO | Retrain for each of four held-out devices; evaluate on that device with paired seeds and separate calibration patients. | Tests device transfer and conformal behavior. |
| KAUH | Run frozen candidate checkpoints on KAUH recordings and paired filter views without target-label tuning. | Tests external recording/filter behavior, not physical stethoscopes. |

No source-CV gain below is evidence of a gain on any of these three endpoints. The class-aware hypothesis further needs a non-class-aware PCSL=5 control to separate the effect of pairing from the changed loss weight.

## Recommended scope of the paper

**Working title:** *Spectrum Correction and Uncertainty-Aware Lung-Sound Classification Under Stethoscope Shift*.

**Study question:** How far can trained static spectral correction and split conformal prediction make a pretrained lung-sound classifier reliable when the recording device changes, and where do they fail?

Keep the frozen BEATs + PAFA comparison as the main model study: no SC versus trained static SC, with the test-time-only SC isolation contrast. Report E1 and E1+E3 as exploratory ensemble extensions. The new class-aware-light result is a secondary, source-only candidate until it receives paired official, LODO, and KAUH evaluation. Do not replace the deployed or frozen primary model on its source-CV result.

This scope supports a methods-and-evaluation contribution. It does not support a claim of state-of-the-art accuracy, proven physical-device robustness, or restored conformal coverage on unseen hardware. The registered v9 AST study and its hypotheses retain their original provenance; the later BEATs selection is explicitly exploratory.

## Methods tied to those questions

| Component | Fixed method |
| --- | --- |
| Data and tasks | ICBHI four-class cycle classification; four leave-one-device-out cohorts; KAUH paired Bell/Diaphragm/Extended software-filter views and recording-level screening. Preserve the committed official split for comparability and also report its unseen-patient subset: patients 156 and 218 occur on both sides of the full official split. Keep source CV, LODO, calibration, and adaptation/evaluation patient-disjoint. |
| Main model | Fine-tuned BEATs with PAFA; trained static SC over the saved 50–2000 Hz common-band reference. Estimate coefficients for a new device from unlabelled target audio. Preserve frozen epochs and paired seeds 0–4. |
| Primary comparison | No-SC BEATs + PAFA versus its trained-static-SC counterpart. Include same-weight test-time-only SC as the isolation contrast. Report per-class recall, Sp, Se, Score, HS, and each device before a macro average. |
| Uncertainty | Split conformal LAC prediction sets at nominal 90% coverage; report observed coverage, coverage error, set size, and uncertain/referral rate. Apply the registered null gate before testing H2. |
| Paired robustness controls | KAUH-A compares four-class predictions on the same 8-second audio rendered through three software filters. KAUH-B2 calibrates on one filter and evaluates another; use the six ordered pairs and 200 source-device null re-splits. |
| Statistical units | Pair seeds and patients; bootstrap whole patients and seeds, with calibration repeated in each draw where required. Do not use target outcomes to select training recipes, epochs, ensemble weights, or adaptation strength. |
| Secondary studies | Equal-weight E1/E1+E3 ensembles; source-selected class-aware-light candidate; target adaptation on disjoint unlabelled adaptation/evaluation patients. Keep their endpoints separate from the zero-shot main comparison. |
